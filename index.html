Frequently Asked Questions

What are your opening hours?+
We're open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — including nights and holidays. Flat tyre at 3 AM? Just drive in or call 052 298 8166.
Do I need an appointment?+
No appointment needed — walk in any time, day or night. You can also send us the details on WhatsApp and we'll be ready for you.
Where are you located?+
We're on Sheikh Zayed Road, Al Quoz, Al Manara Street, Dubai — easy to reach from across the city. Tap the map below for directions.
What services do you offer?+
New tyres (all major brands), alignment & balancing, new rims & alloy wheels, rim repair, batteries, AC service, oil & brake service, mechanic, auto electrics, dent & paint and window tinting.

Michelin, Continental, Pirelli, Goodyear and Bridgestone among others. Call or WhatsApp us your tyre size for a quick quote.
